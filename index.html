Saree Gown

Your Saree, Our Stitching - A Beautiful Gown!

Have an old or used saree lying in your cupboard, or bought a new saree you would love as a gown? Bring it to us. We will stitch a beautiful gown from it at an affordable price.

  1. Bring your saree - old, used or brand new.
  2. Choose a design - show us your idea or pick from our designs.
  3. Perfect measurements - we stitch it for a perfect fit.
  4. Collect your gown and shine at the function!
